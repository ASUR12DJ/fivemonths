Happy five months, my Kittu. 💗

It still makes me smile that our story began in the most random way. One little Instagram follow at 1:25 in the morning, then that night when we talked and talked, laughing, flirting, and somehow turning a random comment section into our little universe.

Five months with you has given me so many moments I want to keep close: your messages popping up on my screen, our late-night talks, the silly arguments, the games where we both get way too competitive, and all the times you make an ordinary day feel softer just by being in it.

Thank you for choosing me, for every paragraph and every little effort, for letting me draw you again and again, and for being exactly you. I love you more than this page can fit, and I hope month five is just one little chapter in a very long story of us. 🥹

All my love, always. 💋
